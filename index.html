Virtual league

Week by week

Each week, teams in a conference are paired head to head on their ACES Score. A win earns 2 points and a tie 1, and any team that beats its seeding baseline earns +1, win or lose. A team with a bye plays the conference average. Pick MN SCTP, or Nationwide to see the league with every team in the country.

Simulated season. These weeks are generated from real shooters' scores to show how the league works. Real results replace them once the season starts.

The algorithm

How the ACES Score works

Most team scores add up the top few shooters. That rewards a team with two stars and a weak bench. The ACES Score looks at the whole spread of a team's 50-target scores and adds five points from its box plot.

ACES= Median+Q3+Q1+Lower whisker+Max
  • MedianThe middle score on the team. Half the scores are above it.
  • Q3Upper quartile: 75% of scores are at or below it. Shows how strong the upper half is.
  • Q1Lower quartile: 25% of scores are at or below it. Shows how solid the lower half is.
  • Lower whiskerQ1 − 1.5 × (Q3 − Q1), but never below the team's lowest score. A long tail of low scores pulls it down.
  • MaxThe team's best 50-target score.

Four of the five numbers describe the middle and bottom of the team. Only one rewards the single best shooter. A team climbs when its newest shooters improve.

Each part tops out at 50, so a perfect score is 250. The parts are statistics, not sums, so a team of 8 and a team of 80 are compared fairly no matter how many rounds each posted. Quartiles use the same inclusive method as Google Sheets QUARTILE.

About

Who we are

The Aitkin County Elite Sharpshooters (ACES) are a youth shooting team in central Minnesota. We created the ACES Score to see how our whole team was doing, and we host these rankings so every team can compare the same way.

We're always looking to add new shooters. If you're interested, reach out.
